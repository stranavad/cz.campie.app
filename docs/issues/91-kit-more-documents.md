## What to build

The templates organisers search for that the first four do not cover. Each is written from the regulations like the first four (issue 89): fixed core, optional items with a `why` Fact, no camp data in sentences.

Ordered by expected search demand and how much the organiser gains:

1. **Přihláška na tábor** - the registration form on paper: participant, guardians, contacts, what the camp needs to know. No rodné číslo by default. The natural bridge to the product, which does the same online.
2. **Informace o zpracování osobních údajů** (čl. 13 GDPR) - the companion of the consent template: purposes, legal bases, recipients, periods, rights. Needs a table block in the document model.
3. **Zdravotní dotazník dítěte** - what the zdravotník needs beyond the posudek. Blocked by open question 1 of issue 90.
4. **Potvrzení o účasti a úhradě** - for an employer or a health insurer. Rodné číslo optional and only with the consent of the consent template.
5. **Plná moc k vyzvednutí dítěte a písemné zmocnění k podpisu prohlášení** - the authorisation § 9 odst. 3 of zákon č. 258/2000 Sb. asks for when someone else than the guardian hands the child over.
6. **Záznam o instruktáži** - the signature sheet proving the briefing of § 11 odst. 2.
7. **Evidence výpisů z rejstříku trestů** - a list of who showed the extract and when, without copies (see open question 3 of issue 90).
8. **Zdravotnický deník** - printable pages; the law prescribes no content, so the columns are advice.
9. **Lékárnička** - příloha č. 4 of the decree as a checklist to tick.
10. **Táborový řád** - a starting text for the camp's own rules.
11. **Potvrzení pro pracovní volno vedoucího** (§ 203a zákoníku práce) - what the employee has to prove to the employer.

Also here: **Word export** of customised templates, if organisers ask for it. The research found that most ranking templates are DOCX; a PDF generated from the organiser's own settings may make it unnecessary.
